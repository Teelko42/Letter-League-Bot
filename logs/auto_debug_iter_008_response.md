The loop hit its 10-click cap and burned ~22 s on every rejected word in `_recall_tiles`, plus ~20 s at the start of every turn in `clear_stale_placements`. Five rejections × ~22 s ≈ 110 s of pure waste per turn, which (combined with valid placement work) pushed the run past the auto-debug 30-min subprocess wallclock and triggered SIGKILL (exit -9). Files changed: `src/browser/tile_placer.py` only — `_recall_tiles` now takes `expected_tiles` and clicks recall exactly `tiles + 2` times with no diff check (~7 s instead of ~22 s); `place_move` passes `len(move.rack_tiles_consumed())`; `clear_stale_placements` switched to a fixed 4-click cap on the same rationale. Tests: 23/23 pass in `tests/test_tile_placer.py`. Why next run does better: a 5-rejection turn now spends ~35 s in recall instead of ~110 s — roughly 75 s saved per painful turn — letting the bot complete its 5-turn quota inside the 30-min cap with substantial headroom.
